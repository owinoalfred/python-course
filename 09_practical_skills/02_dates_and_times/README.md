# 9.2 — Dates and Times

**Module 9: Intermediate Practical Skills and Best Practices**

datetime, date, time, timedelta, timezone awareness, and ISO-8601 formatting.

## Why this topic matters

Accurate timestamping and timezone handling are critical for robot telemetry streams.

## Learning objectives

1. Parse and format ISO-8601 dates.
2. Perform date arithmetic with timedelta.
3. Handle UTC and timezone awareness.
