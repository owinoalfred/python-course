# Instructor notes — 8.4 CRUD Operations

**Module 8: Databases**

## Teaching objectives

- Teach core concept of CRUD Operations.
- Guide students through edge cases.

## Likely misconceptions

| Misconception | Correction to drive home |
| --- | --- |
| Thinking CRUD Operations is static | Clarify dynamic runtime execution in CPython |

## Difficult concepts

- Understanding edge-case handling in CRUD Operations.

## Demonstration suggestions

- Live demo of CRUD Operations in python REPL.

## Discussion questions

- Why is CRUD Operations preferred in this context?

## Common student errors

| Error | Why it happens | Intervention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IndexError / KeyError in CRUD Operations | Accessing elements without bounds check | Add length check or try-except |

2 hours lecture + 3 hours lab exercises.

## Extension activities
