# Instructor notes — 3.7 Comprehensions

**Module 3: Core Data Structures**

## Teaching objectives

- Teach core concept of Comprehensions.
- Guide students through edge cases.

## Likely misconceptions

| Misconception | Correction to drive home |
| --- | --- |
| Thinking Comprehensions is static | Clarify dynamic runtime execution in CPython |

## Difficult concepts

- Understanding edge-case handling in Comprehensions.

## Demonstration suggestions

- Live demo of Comprehensions in python REPL.

## Discussion questions

- Why is Comprehensions preferred in this context?

## Common student errors

| Error | Why it happens | Intervention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IndexError / KeyError in Comprehensions | Accessing elements without bounds check | Add length check or try-except |

2 hours lecture + 3 hours lab exercises.

## Extension activities
