# Instructor notes — 4.1 Defining and Calling Functions

**Module 4: Functions and Modules**

## Teaching objectives

- Teach core concept of Defining and Calling Functions.
- Guide students through edge cases.

## Likely misconceptions

| Misconception | Correction to drive home |
| --- | --- |
| Thinking Defining and Calling Functions is static | Clarify dynamic runtime execution in CPython |

## Difficult concepts

- Understanding edge-case handling in Defining and Calling Functions.

## Demonstration suggestions

- Live demo of Defining and Calling Functions in python REPL.

## Discussion questions

## Common student errors

| Error | Why it happens | Intervention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IndexError / KeyError in Defining and Calling Functions | Accessing elements without bounds check | Add length check or try-except |
