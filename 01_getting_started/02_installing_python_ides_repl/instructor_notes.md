# Instructor notes — 1.2 Installing Python, IDEs, and the REPL

**Module 1: Getting Started with Python**

## Teaching objectives

- Teach core concept of Installing Python, IDEs, and the REPL.
- Guide students through edge cases.

## Likely misconceptions

| Misconception | Correction to drive home |
| --- | --- |
| Thinking Installing Python, IDEs, and the REPL is static | Clarify dynamic runtime execution in CPython |

## Difficult concepts

- Understanding edge-case handling in Installing Python, IDEs, and the REPL.

## Demonstration suggestions

- Live demo of Installing Python, IDEs, and the REPL in python REPL.

## Discussion questions

- Why is Installing Python, IDEs, and the REPL preferred in this context?

## Common student errors

| Error | Why it happens | Intervention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IndexError / KeyError in Installing Python, IDEs, and the REPL | Accessing elements without bounds check | Add length check or try-except |

## Recommended pacing

2 hours lecture + 3 hours lab exercises.

## Extension activities

- Advanced optimization profiling.
