# Instructor notes — 1.6 Operators and Expressions

**Module 1: Getting Started with Python**

## Teaching objectives

- Teach core concept of Operators and Expressions.
- Guide students through edge cases.

## Likely misconceptions

| Misconception | Correction to drive home |
| --- | --- |
| Thinking Operators and Expressions is static | Clarify dynamic runtime execution in CPython |

## Difficult concepts

- Understanding edge-case handling in Operators and Expressions.

## Demonstration suggestions

- Live demo of Operators and Expressions in python REPL.

## Discussion questions

- Why is Operators and Expressions preferred in this context?

## Common student errors

| Error | Why it happens | Intervention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IndexError / KeyError in Operators and Expressions | Accessing elements without bounds check | Add length check or try-except |
