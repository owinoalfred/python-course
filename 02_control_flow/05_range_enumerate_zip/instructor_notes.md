# Instructor notes — 2.5 range(), enumerate(), and zip()

**Module 2: Control Flow and Loops**

## Teaching objectives

- Teach core concept of range(), enumerate(), and zip().
- Guide students through edge cases.

## Likely misconceptions

| Misconception | Correction to drive home |
| --- | --- |
| Thinking range(), enumerate(), and zip() is static | Clarify dynamic runtime execution in CPython |

## Difficult concepts

- Understanding edge-case handling in range(), enumerate(), and zip().

## Demonstration suggestions

- Live demo of range(), enumerate(), and zip() in python REPL.

## Discussion questions

- Why is range(), enumerate(), and zip() preferred in this context?

## Common student errors

| Error | Why it happens | Intervention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IndexError / KeyError in range(), enumerate(), and zip() | Accessing elements without bounds check | Add length check or try-except |
