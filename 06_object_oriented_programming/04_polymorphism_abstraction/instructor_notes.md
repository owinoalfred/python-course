# Instructor notes — 6.4 Polymorphism and Abstraction

**Module 6: Object-Oriented Programming**

## Teaching objectives

- Teach core concept of Polymorphism and Abstraction.
- Guide students through edge cases.

## Likely misconceptions

| Misconception | Correction to drive home |
| --- | --- |
| Thinking Polymorphism and Abstraction is static | Clarify dynamic runtime execution in CPython |

## Difficult concepts

- Understanding edge-case handling in Polymorphism and Abstraction.

## Demonstration suggestions

- Live demo of Polymorphism and Abstraction in python REPL.

## Discussion questions

- Why is Polymorphism and Abstraction preferred in this context?

## Common student errors

| Error | Why it happens | Intervention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IndexError / KeyError in Polymorphism and Abstraction | Accessing elements without bounds check | Add length check or try-except |

## Recommended pacing

2 hours lecture + 3 hours lab exercises.

## Extension activities

- Advanced optimization profiling.
